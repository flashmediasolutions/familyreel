Scanner compatibility

FamilyReel works with network scanners and all-in-one printers that support AirScan, also called eSCL: the driver-free scanning standard that most current network models use. You don’t install anything from the scanner’s maker.

Use as many as you have at the same time. Each one gets its own card and Scan button, and each one adds its own pace.

Should work

Most current network all-in-one printers and scanners from HP, Epson, Canon and Brother support AirScan (eSCL), and should work the same way. Brands interpret the standard a little differently, so if yours misbehaves, tell us and we’ll look into it.

How to check your scanner

  1. Try FamilyReel free. It finds AirScan scanners on your network within seconds, and your first 100 photos are free. If it finds yours and scans, you’re set.
  2. Ask your Mac. If you can scan from the printer with macOS’s own Image Capture app, or from System Settings › Printers & Scanners, without having installed the maker’s software, it almost certainly speaks AirScan.
  3. Read the spec sheet. Look for “AirScan”, “eSCL”, “Mopria Scan” or scanning through “AirPrint”.

Not supported in this version

  • Scanners connected only by USB, with no network connection.
  • Sheet feeders and document feeders: FamilyReel always scans the glass, which is kinder to old prints.
  • Film, slide and negative scanning.
  • Scanners on a different network from the Mac, such as a guest Wi-Fi.
