Privacy policy

FamilyReel for Mac, from Flash Media Solutions, Inc. · Effective November 22, 2026

In short: FamilyReel collects no data. Your scans, photos, slideshows and music stay on your Mac. There’s no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We never see your photos.

What stays on your Mac

Everything FamilyReel makes or uses stays on your Mac, in the folder you choose (Pictures › FamilyReel unless you pick another) and in FamilyReel’s own storage on your Mac:

  • your scans, the photos cut from them and their color-restored copies;
  • dates, captions and the order of your photos;
  • slideshows, the music you add to them and the movies you export;
  • your settings and the list of your scanners.

Restoring colors, straightening photos, guessing dates and making movies all happen on your Mac. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else for processing.

Your network

FamilyReel uses your local network only to find your scanners and talk to them: it sends a scan job and receives the scanned image. macOS asks your permission for this the first time. FamilyReel doesn’t contact any server of ours, because there isn’t one.

No analytics, no tracking, no ads

FamilyReel contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code and no third-party services. It doesn’t track you across apps or websites, and it doesn’t build any profile of you.

Send Report

If something goes wrong, you can choose Help › Send Report…. FamilyReel shows you exactly what it would include: the app version, your macOS version and Mac model, your scanner models and FamilyReel’s recent log messages. Then it opens an email draft in your own email app. Nothing is sent unless you send that email yourself. We use what you send only to answer you, and we delete it when you ask.

The same goes for any email you write to us: we use it only to reply.

Purchases

FamilyReel Unlimited is sold through the Mac App Store, and Apple handles the payment. We never see your payment details or your Apple Account. Apple’s StoreKit tells FamilyReel, on your Mac, whether FamilyReel Unlimited is unlocked. Apple’s own privacy policy covers your purchase.

Crash reports from Apple

If you have chosen, in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements, to share analytics with app developers, Apple may pass us anonymous crash reports for FamilyReel. That is Apple’s setting, off unless you turned it on, and those reports don’t identify you.

Children

FamilyReel collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. A change that would collect any data would be announced in the app first.
