FamilyReel: Scan & Slideshow · for Mac

Every scanner you own, at once.

FamilyReel turns the all-in-one printers in your house into one photo-scanning line. While one scans, you load the next. Then it turns the whole box of prints into a slideshow with music.

Measured in our first big scanning session, at 300 dpi: about 12 photos a minute, with each scanner keeping its own pace of about 4. One scanner works too, and a second and third really add up.

How it works

  1. Turn on every scanner you have. FamilyReel finds the scanners on your network and gives each one its own card, its own Scan button and its own shortcut.
  2. Lay prints on every glass and press Scan All. Several prints per scan is fine; leave a little space around each one.
  3. While one scans, load the next. A Next marker shows where to walk, and status is big enough to read from across the room. No scanner ever waits for processing.
  4. Press Play. Your photos, cut out, straightened, restored and in order, become a slideshow with music.

Everything the box of prints needs

  • Several scanners at once

    Each scanner has a Scan button and a shortcut (⌘1 to ⌘9), plus Scan All. Optional auto-scan starts the next scan by itself, and pauses instead of saving an empty glass or prints you haven’t swapped yet.

  • Cut and straighten

    Every print on the glass is found, cut out, straightened and turned the right way up. Photos it isn’t sure about are counted for later and never stop the line.

  • Faded color, back

    Prints that turned orange or magenta get their color back, with dust specks cleaned, in a separate Restored copy. Your originals stay exactly as scanned.

  • In order, by best guess

    Black-and-white prints first, faded 1970s and 80s prints next, newer photos after, and dated phone photos where they belong. Drag anything you know better.

  • A slideshow with music

    Pan and zoom, crossfades and title cards. Add songs you own and FamilyReel evens out their volume. Play full screen, or, with FamilyReel Unlimited, export a movie for the TV, the family or email.

  • A gentle ending

    For a memorial or a milestone: close the show on the photo you choose, held as long as you like, with a two-line closing title.

Everything stays on your Mac

Scanning, restoring, ordering and making movies all happen on your Mac. No account, no uploads, no ads, no tracking: we never see your photos. They’re ordinary image files in a folder you choose. Read the privacy policy.

Free for your first 100 photos

Try FamilyReel with your own scanners, free, for up to 100 photos. When you’re ready, one purchase, FamilyReel Unlimited, removes the limit and unlocks movie export, for good. It works with Family Sharing, so your family can use it too.

What you need

  • A Mac.
  • A network scanner or all-in-one printer that supports AirScan (eSCL), on the same network as your Mac. Most current network models do. Check your scanner.
  • No scanner nearby? Choose Help › Demo Mode in the app to try everything with sample scanners and sample prints.
